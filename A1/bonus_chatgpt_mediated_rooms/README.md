# Bonus ChatGPT trial — mediated rooms

These three images are supplementary visual tests for Sam Hoyek's in-progress experimental music and visual project, *demonstration_02: soma on_affect*: `Buffered Touch`, `Split-Room Body`, and `Absent Call`. Sam previously selected them within a D2 visual-language baseline.

Each JPG is paired with a TXT file containing a saved **retrospective summary** of the image direction. The exact prompts sent to ChatGPT were not recovered, and no generation seeds are known. These images were made with ChatGPT image generation, not Z-Image, MFLUX, or Diffusers. They are included to show a related artistic experiment, not to substitute for the main Z-Image series or claim reproducibility that the records cannot support.
